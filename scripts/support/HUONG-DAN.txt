1. Tren may da gap loi, dung thu thap va dong ung dung TrendLink binh thuong.
2. Giai nen goi lay log ra mot thu muc. Khong chay truc tiep ben trong ZIP.
3. Nhan dup LAY-LOG-TRENDLINK.cmd. Khong can quyen Administrator hay cai Node.js.
4. Cho den khi hien DA TAO. File TrendLink-logs-...zip nam tren Desktop.
5. Gui file TrendLink-logs-...zip vao cuoc tro chuyen de phan tich.

Cong cu doc trang thai TrendLink va dong goi log chan doan.
Chi gom trang thai, ma loi, log thu thap, cau hinh, prompt va tu khoa cua cac luot gan day.
Khong gom browser, secrets, cookie, bien moi truong hay toan bo bai viet.
Neu du lieu da bi xoa, cong cu khong khoi phuc duoc log cua nhung luot da mat.
Neu bao loi, chup lai cua so thong bao va gui vao cuoc tro chuyen.
